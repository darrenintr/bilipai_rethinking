route triggerPrefetch logging through diagLog so it survives diagnostic tail truncation


Build 327 confirmed the qn-fallback fix ran (video 2 reached
Playback manifest ready and serve returned) but the trigger log
lines were invisible in the diagnostic. Root cause:
Logger.shared.logs is capped at 1000 entries, and the
diagnostic tail only shows the last 100 via suffix 100. A
hot-path video emits several hundred loadedTimeRanges and
upstream response lines between the trigger and the export,
pushing the trigger entry out of the window.

Switch the trigger log to diagLog .playback so the entry
lands in DiagnosticLogger.events and the on-disk log.jsonl,
both of which are dumped verbatim into the Diagnostic
events section of the diagnostic report. Also add a
diagog entry at PlaybackPrefetchManager.prefetch entry so
we can confirm the spawn actually reaches the actor and
which of cache-hit vs in-flight vs new prefetch path ran.

Dump bvid, cid, requested qn, resolved qn, acceptQuality
first, and hasDash on both the skipped and firing paths so
the next diagnostic is self-explanatory.
